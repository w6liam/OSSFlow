UtterDroid

Open source voice dictation

Speak. It types. You keep it.

UtterDroid is open source voice dictation. You bring your own AI keys. There is no account.

You own 100% of your data.

Your own AI keys Transcription and cleanup run on the models you choose.
No login No account to create, and nothing to sign in to.
Nothing in the cloud UtterDroid does not keep a copy of what you say or write.

How it works

  1. 1Tap the bubble.
  2. 2Speak.
  3. 3Tap again. The text lands in the field you were using.

Audio is deleted after transcription. History stays on the phone.
